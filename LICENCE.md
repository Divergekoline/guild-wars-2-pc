## 🧩 About

**Guild Wars 2 Hack** is a comprehensive overlay menu for Guild Wars 2 featuring waypoint teleport, speed control, dps meter, inventory helper, and camera tools.

Based on popular overlays like **ArcDPS**, **GW2TaCo**, and **BlisHUD**.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- Waypoint Teleport — jump to any unlocked waypoint instantly
- Speed Control — adjust movement speed (0.5x to 3x)
- No Clip Camera — free camera for exploring maps
- Player ESP — highlight allies, enemies, and NPCs
- Auto Loot — collect drops in a radius

### 🎨 Interface & Overlay
- Overlay Menu — in-game configurable overlay panel
- DPS Meter — real-time damage and healing tracking
- Minimap Markers — custom markers and route waypoints
- Inventory Helper — search, sort, and preview inventory items

### 🏋️ Utility & Training
- Teleport to Coordinates — set custom map coordinates
- Auto Dodge — assist with dodge timing in combat
- Build Templates — quick swap trait and gear templates
- Coordinate Display — show current map coordinates (x, y, z)
